# Sessions

Every agent session's life, as the warden runs it: who may start and on what, launching and resuming sessions,
benching, reviving and retiring them (a session that outlived an upgrade is told to load its skills again, and comes
back as a copy when its subagents changed), the watch over running ones, the backstop against a session the warden
did not start, reaping what a retired session left, its registry, its subagents, its settings, its limits, and the
sentence that says what it is doing.
