# Replays

A tester's run, made into the replay the owner watches on the dashboard: each item it tested, each step's expected
and got, and every thought and action, played over the pictures of the screen at the run's own pace.

The warden builds it after the run, off its tick, from what the run left: its browsers' traces, the tester's
transcript, its step marks and its findings, and the scenarios as the tested build holds them. Nothing slows the
tester. No secret reaches the replay: a sign-in link or the desktop's password is masked in its words, and the
picture is hidden for a while after one is typed. The traces are deleted once the replay is built. A run whose tester
marked no steps, as a regression tester's, plays as one recording. When the tester used its second browser, the
replay shows the browser it was acting in and says when that is the second; so does each screenshot. A run that
cannot be replayed says why in its place.
